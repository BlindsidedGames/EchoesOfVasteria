# Lock selection

With the lock selection tool you can lock an area to prevent it from accidentally being edited or deleted. To lock a selection select the area you want to lock with the [[Editor Tools#Selection tool|selection tool]], then click the **lock selection** button in [[Better Rule Tile Editor#The toolbar|the toolbar]] or by using [[Keyboard shortcuts|the L keyboard shortcut]].

![[lock-selection.png]]

When a selection is locked, all the locked cells will be displayed with a red outline by default. You can change how the locked cells look in the [[Better Rule Tile Editor#Editor settings|editor settings]].

When a cell is locked, it won't allow you to modify the sprite or tile with the [[Editor Tools#Brush tool|brush]] or [[Editor Tools#Eraser tool|eraser]] tools, and you won't be able to move them with the [[Editor Tools#Move tool|move tool]] or delete them when pressing **Delete**.

![[move-locked-selection.gif]]

# Unlock selection

To unlock a selection just simply select them with the [[Editor Tools#Selection tool|selection tool]] again and click the **Unlock selection** button in [[Better Rule Tile Editor#The toolbar|the toolbar]], which is located right next to the **Lock selection** button. You can also use [[Keyboard shortcuts|the U keyboard shortcut]].

## Locked cell options

The appearance of the locked cells can be customized in the [[Better Rule Tile Editor#Editor settings|editor settings]] window. The available options are the following:

- **Show locked overlay** - Adds a striped overlay on top of locked cells.
- **Show locked outline** - Displays an outline around individual locked cells.
- **Outline color** - The outline color of the locked cells.
