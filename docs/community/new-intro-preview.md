# A new beginning in Echoes of Vasteria

A look at the reworked opening, from your first quest to forging your first sword.

Hey everyone!

I've been reworking the opening of Echoes of Vasteria so that your first adventures give you a clearer introduction to the world, its characters, and the systems you'll use along the way.

Here's a look at the new intro, all the way through to crafting and equipping your first sword. The quiet stretches have been shortened or sped up for the video, with the playback speed shown in the bottom-right corner.

[Watch the new intro](https://youtu.be/IRcvrl5uYxY)

## Your first steps
Orin now welcomes you to town and introduces your quest panel. Eva gives you a magic watering can and a reason to head into the Farmlands: gather some radishes!

That first trip is a little gentler, with fewer tasks along the route. You'll meet Carl, learn why there's a limit to how far you can travel, and find Gill fishing by the water.

## Meeting people, opening new paths
Gill's introduction leads you towards the River, where you'll meet Barkley, before continuing on to the Woods and Ivan. The early quests have been rearranged to give those encounters a clearer order.

Returning to town also has more purpose. Gill's Old Boots give you your first equipment reward, followed by a short introduction to your inventory and equipment bonuses. Other lessons appear as their systems become relevant, instead of arriving all at once.

## Forge your first sword
Ivan now teaches you how to make your first sword. He supplies the starting materials, and the tutorial walks you through smelting an ingot, choosing a core, crafting the weapon, and equipping it.

It also introduces how cores affect your chances of crafting different rarities, giving you a starting point for experimenting with the forge later on.

## A clearer view of your progress
I've also been tidying up the UI around those first adventures:
- Hand prompts point out the next useful button.
- Menus are introduced gradually as you need them.
- The run summary gives your resources and reaping distance a clearer layout.
- Your inventory shows equipped items and their stat bonuses.
- A compact in-run stats panel keeps extra information close without crowding the screen.

This is a preview of the opening as it stands in development. I'd love to hear what you think: does the order feel natural, and is there anything that still needs a little more explanation?

Thanks for following along!
