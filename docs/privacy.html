Privacy Policy

Echoes of Vasteria respects your privacy. This page explains what limited data we collect and why.

Diagnostics Only

We collect Unity diagnostics data strictly to help us find and fix crashes and stability issues. This may include crash reports, error logs, basic device and OS information, and the game version.

No personal data: We do not collect or retain information that directly identifies you. Diagnostic data is used only to improve the game and is never used for marketing or profiling.

No Ads or Behavioral Tracking

We do not serve ads, run behavioral tracking, or collect gameplay analytics. Outside of crash/diagnostic telemetry, your gameplay stays on your device.

Local Data Storage

Game progress and settings are stored locally on your device. This data is only accessible to you and is not shared with us or any third parties.

Third-Party Services

Diagnostics are processed by Unity on our behalf (e.g., crash reporting). For details on Unity’s handling of diagnostic data, see Unity’s Privacy Policy: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy.

If you purchase the game through Steam, please refer to Steam’s privacy policy for how they handle your store/account data.

Contact

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the Steam community page for Echoes of Vasteria.
