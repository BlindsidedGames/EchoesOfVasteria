## Acceptance gates

- Every fresh/legacy pack starts zero/undiscovered independent of old crop unlocks; first committed new-version pack discovery persists at zero balance. Unknown UI leaks no crop names/icons.
- Each pack pays for one batch. Double planting/harvest, repeated foreground events and repeated migration cannot duplicate value.
- Online and offline time lead the same paid batch to the same Ready state. Long absence yields no additional batch. Ready crops persist without loss.
- Invalid/future/backward timestamps and nonfinite values never grant negative costs, negative progress or unlimited output.
- Aggregate material costs are verified before any debit. Insufficient materials, failed writes and interrupted commits retain recoverable state.
- Slot changes and delayed callbacks cannot move seeds, crops, claims or unlocks into another slot.
- Unknown resource/recipe IDs remain recoverable; legacy stored balances, card counts, completed quests and collection history have explicit outcomes.
- Harvest does not increase BestPerMinute, adventure gathering totals, tier rolls or seed drop acquisition. Resource-tier yield is applied once.
- Harvest does not grant repeatable Farming XP; concurrent adventure rewards, quest hand-ins and autosave during commits retain all legitimate mutations.
- Retired generation stops across all categories. Every ordinary/grouped/lowest-count card pool handles all retired generation cards consistently.
- Cauldron roll distributions, tasting effort and Infinity timing meet the approved post-retirement design.
- Fresh, midgame and advanced saves can reach the final farm without relying on resources removed by retirement. Completed old quests are not charged again; new authored build requirements apply equally, without automatic carryover/waivers.
- New expansion quests preserve approved original-completion/leaderboard semantics. No automatic Fence→farm grant or grandfathered crop access; derived requirements are identical for fresh/legacy players.
- All actions work without UGS, Steam or feedback delivery. Conflicting valid save branches retain explicit recovery rather than automatic value merging.
- Normal-scale crops/fences/path openings remain readable, buildings/NPC approaches unobstructed, and world clicks have equivalent list actions.
- Keyboard navigation, long localized strings, 1024-width and ultrawide screens, Mac high DPI and reduced motion receive actual checks.
