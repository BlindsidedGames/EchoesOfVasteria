# Copy audit — three revised farming screens

Every text layer in the revised desktop, narrow and seed/bed detail identifies an item/control, shows a value/state or names an action. No other text remains. The complete per-string classification is in [revision verification](revision-1-verification.json).

Removed: the invented twins’ quote; STATIC CONCEPT and illustrative/undecided labels; adventure-discovery subtitles; the explanation of `???`; repeated `Bed N` beside an existing bed number; duplicate planting controls; “Choose a seed”, “Choose another seed” and batch/world-change instructions; the duplicate Discovered state; unknown-slot quantity dashes; decorative capacity subtitles; and all bottom design disclaimers.

Retained: navigation and close controls; NPC names, level and XP values; Seeds/Beds/Garden headings; packet identities and owned quantities; the shared unknown icon with `???`; bed numbers, Ready/Growing/Empty/Locked states and remaining time; Harvest/Details/Focus garden/Back actions; one Plant commit; the selected seed/bed value; and narrow page controls plus its page value. Each supports selection, navigation, action or a meaningful state. Locked remains the unavailable-bed reason; no invented requirement or unlock cost is substituted.

`Ready to harvest` becomes `Ready` beside Harvest. `Growing · 12 min left` becomes `Growing · 12 min`. The narrow selected empty bed retains `Radish · 6 packs` so its Plant action has a visible seed target. Desktop instead shows `Radish → Bed 3` next to its single Plant button. Detail identifies Bed 3 and Radish before its Plant action. These labels do not approve pack cost, growth timing or XP mechanics.

Actual Forge/Cauldron references and all three final saved renders were inspected. Packet label/count spacing is tightened within the existing cards; the desktop’s world number markers are enlarged. The visual component language remains unchanged. All design assumptions are in the accompanying notes, outside the three interfaces.
