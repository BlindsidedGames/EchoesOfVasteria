# Approved unlock and stat-data omission contract

User decision, 1 October 2026: **everyone follows the current game-defined unlock format. No grandfathered old adventure crop access. Discard obsolete per-save unlock requirements/derived unlock data and purchased stat upgrades from the new format, without gear compensation. Retain actual skill levels/XP and meaningful quest progress.** Everyone discovers new seed packs fresh; no Alter Echo compensation remains approved.

Planning and isolated serialization only. Production code, migrations, scenes and original saves are unchanged. Keeping an untouched premigration backup is distinct from carrying obsolete fields into every new save.

## Nonderivable data and ambiguity

**The current maximum remains five regular buff slots:** one initial slot plus Mew/BuffSlot2, Seriously?/BuffSlot3, Just Keep Eating./BuffSlot4, and A lap, and a Feast/BuffSlot5, each +1 in sequence. A Fishy Situation/Mildred is the prerequisite buff-unlock quest and adds no slot. This is a historical-ID migration issue, not a reduced maximum.

Fixture es3-36 stores two regular and two auto slots; Mildred and Mildred1 are completed, while BuffSlot2–5 are absent. Raw current-ID reconstruction gives one regular slot. History now establishes the missing alias: d1729403f (2 August 2025) renames Another Fishy Situation, questId Mildred1, to BuffSlot2. The asset GUID a04629c010952d741b32db280e62a8eb, +1-slot reward, prerequisite and fish requirement remain the same at that transition. Current display name is Mew; its amount later rose from 50 to 100.

On a disposable copy, translating that completed receipt to BuffSlot2 makes current reconstruction equal the saved two slots; completing the remaining three current quests reaches five. Without the alias, the current Mew quest could reacquire slot two, but an already-paid receipt would be ignored and require another payment. **Recommend preserving the proven receipt alias before deriving capacity**, without executing CompleteQuest, spending fish or replaying rewards. Do not preserve obsolete requirements or invent a completion from the count alone. Other historical IDs still need validation before claiming universal reconstruction; keep/flag unmatched state until resolved. The fixture's platform provenance remains unproven. [buff-slot-history.json](evidence/buff-slot-history.json) records the exact chain and experiment.

Incomplete legacy distance objectives need removed-baseline adapters. Current definitions can replace targets, but progress since acceptance is player history. Translate where historical rules are known, or flag the affected objective; no blanket quest reset is approved. Resource discovery and active milestone choices likewise cannot derive from definitions alone.

No Onion→Funion conversion follows: both existed together. Meaningful unknown inventory/history still needs safe handling. Obsolete purchases/eligibility metadata should not be copied into a legacy block in every new payload; source banks/one-time backups supply recovery evidence.

## Legacy read, then intentional omission

1. Decode by source format/representation into a compatibility DTO. Preserve bytes/provenance; do not assign a modern schema/version through constructor defaults.
2. Validate levels/XP, inventory, meaningful quests and selections. Retire old purchases directly; never run the removed stat-to-gear converter. Correct Cauldron thresholds independently.
3. Project into a new DTO without UpgradeLevels, obsolete guards, old automatic milestone strings, TierIndex or requirement metadata. Retain Level/XP and meaningful selections. Current definitions govern all task/drop/milestone eligibility; no grandfather entitlement or pack grant.
4. Record the transition in the ledger and publish through verified save authority. Reload cannot replay old payouts/conversion/cleanup. Keep recovery snapshots outside routine payloads.

Implementation must update GameData/StaticReferences, compatibility DTO/formatters, SaveMigrationRunner and legacy migrations, ConsoleCommands, and SkillController capture/load together. Merely clearing a list/dictionary leaves its serialized field; removing a property while retaining a backing field can also retain bytes. Current definition requirements are already absent from GameData, so they offer no additional current save-size savings.

## Isolated experiment and acceptance

[run-omission-probes.py](evidence/run-omission-probes.py) generates compatible and future DTO variants outside the repo. The future model omits UpgradeLevels, StatUpgradesMigratedToGear, DuckHelmetSanitized, old milestone records/strings and TierIndex. A per-skill ActiveMilestoneIds set retains only player choices; absent IDs are inactive. Experimental schema 4 is a disposable proposed value, not a committed release/schema reservation.

Checks cover exact levels/XP float bits, typed Id/IsActive values, canonical represented quest/inventory/NPC/loadout/gear/cauldron/statistics values, and omitted-field absence in object/payload. Equipment equality establishes no starter gear was added. The compatibility stage does not solve removed historical objectives; roundtrip equality is not complete raw old-field preservation, native migration or durable publication evidence.

**Recorded result:** 48 projections passed (45 ES3-derived entries, two historical binary-derived entries, one typed 1.4.3 entry). All 228 populated skill records retain exact levels/XP. The 23 typed choice values are equivalent after reload while only five active IDs persist; automatic/inactive milestone records, old strings, TierIndex and stat-purchase/guard fields are absent from the future payload. Represented quests, NPC history, inventory, equipment, capacities/loadout and Cauldron/statistics values remain equal. [omission-results.json](evidence/omission-results.json) records the checks. These counts include related/duplicate backups, not 48 independent released-platform cohorts.

Production acceptance: all supported representations load; current definitions govern eligibility; actual progress and receipts survive; omitted fields stay absent after save/reload; invalid old selections are handled deterministically; spent discovered resources stay discovered; no compensation appears; interrupted/repeated migration cannot replay rewards/sanitation; ambiguous capacity/objective state is retained or handled by an explicit decision. Native upgrades, old asset-dependent migrations and publication remain separate gates.
