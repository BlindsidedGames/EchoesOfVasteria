About the Game

Echoes of Vatheria is a pixel-art idle RPG about building a stronger adventurer. Choose your equipment, combine card effects and spend resources on lasting upgrades.

Complete quests and study your runs through detailed graphs. Play in short sessions or leave the adventure running while you plan your next step.

Game Features

⚔️

Automatic Adventures

Your hero walks, fights and gathers automatically while you manage the build.

🔨

Forge and Refine

Craft gear, compare rolls and refine its stats to prepare for the next run.

🌳

Fields and Cauldron

Grow ingredients in the Fields and brew cards in the Cauldron.

📋

Quests and Progress

Complete quests, unlock lasting upgrades and inspect your progress in detailed graphs.
