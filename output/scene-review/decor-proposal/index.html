How I would place them

Vary groups from single stems to small mixed clumps, with longer bare stretches between them. Use different groups on opposite banks. Put lilies in quieter pools, rocks at selected bends, and fallen timber near the logging area. Keep blue ripple pixels wholly inside water and preserve ground-contact sorting. Use dry grass and occasional flowers to blend the wet edge into the land.

The prefabs use AnimatedDecor to play the animated tiles’ shared frame arrays, with staggered starts and speed variation. They preserve free positioning and renderer sorting. Set ground-contact sorting when placing them; no scene objects were replaced. The 4 FPS gallery preview is close to the prefabs’ 3.4–4.6 FPS range.
