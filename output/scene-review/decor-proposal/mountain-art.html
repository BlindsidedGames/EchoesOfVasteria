How I would dress it

Green terraces: spruce groups near wide corners, dry rocks at cliff feet, and quiet stretches of grass along the route. Snowline: mix regular and snowy spruces on their matching ground. Summit: fewer, stronger tree groups and exposed stone; keep the snowy skyline open. Corrupted bank: dead vegetation toward the brown edge, small purple fungi closer to the stream, and a few mineral rocks as focal points. Mine approaches: restrained working props around access points.

Holiday ornaments, giant mushroom colonies and blue-ripple water props are outside this proposed palette. These are available artwork choices, not newly generated assets.
